Choosing a Funeral Home

Choosing a funeral home is a significant decision, often made under emotional pressure and time constraints. Knowing what to look for helps you choose with confidence.

What funeral homes do

Funeral homes help families handle the practical and ceremonial aspects of a death, care of the body, arranging services, paperwork, and coordination with cemeteries or crematories. Services and prices vary widely, so it's worth understanding your options.

What to look for

  • Clear, itemized pricing (you have a right to a general price list)
  • Compassionate, unhurried staff who listen to your wishes
  • Flexibility to personalize services rather than pushing packages
  • Willingness to accommodate cultural or religious traditions

Questions to ask

  • Can I see an itemized price list?
  • What packages and individual options do you offer?
  • Can we personalize the service the way we'd like?
  • Do you support livestreaming or online memorials for distant family?
  • What are my options for burial, cremation, and other choices?

Comparing your options

Prices for identical services can differ substantially between providers. If time allows, comparing a few funeral homes can save significant money and help you find one whose approach fits your family.

Complementing the service

Many families pair a funeral home's services with an online memorial so friends and relatives who can't attend can still take part and share memories. Ask whether the funeral home supports this, or set one up independently.
