Memorial Service Ideas

A memorial service is a chance to reflect the person at its center. These ideas, readings, rituals, music, and personal touches, help you build something meaningful.

Readings and words

Poems, scripture, song lyrics, or a passage from a book the person loved can anchor a service. Invite a few people to speak, and consider mixing prepared eulogies with open sharing so unexpected voices are heard.

Music

Music sets the emotional tone more than almost anything. Choose songs that meant something to the person, whether that's a hymn, a favorite band, or a piece that simply reminds everyone of them. Live music can add intimacy.

Rituals and symbolic acts

  • Candle lighting, one flame passed among attendees
  • Releasing flowers into water
  • Planting a tree in the person's memory
  • A moment of shared silence
  • Writing messages that are later gathered for the family

Personal touches

  • A memory table with photographs and meaningful objects
  • A slideshow or video montage of their life
  • Displaying their art, work, or collections
  • Serving their favorite foods
  • Asking guests to wear their favorite color

Including everyone

Livestreaming and an online memorial let distant loved ones take part and contribute their own memories. Combining an in-person service with an online tribute means the service lives on and keeps collecting memories afterward.
