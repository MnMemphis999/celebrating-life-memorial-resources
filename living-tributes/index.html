Living Tributes

A living tribute flips the traditional timeline of remembrance. Instead of waiting until someone is gone to gather the stories, photos, and words of love, a living tribute delivers all of that while the person is still here to receive it.

Whether it takes the form of a gathering, a memory book, a video montage, or an online page family and friends contribute to, the goal is the same: to make sure someone hears how much they've meant before it's too late to say it.
