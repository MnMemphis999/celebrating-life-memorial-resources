Ways to Celebrate Someone While They're Here

You don't need a special occasion, or a diagnosis, to tell someone how much they matter. Here are meaningful ways to celebrate a person while they're still here to feel it.

Simple gestures

  • Write a heartfelt letter naming specific things you admire about them
  • Record a short video telling them what they mean to you
  • Make a phone call with no agenda other than appreciation
  • Create a playlist of songs that remind you of them

Gatherings and events

  • Host a 'this is your life' dinner where guests share favorite memories
  • Throw a milestone celebration for a birthday, retirement, or anniversary
  • Organize a surprise gathering of people from different chapters of their life
  • Plan a living tribute or living funeral centered on them

Lasting keepsakes

  • Compile a memory book with letters and photos from many people
  • Interview them on camera about their life and the lessons they've learned
  • Build a shared online tribute page where loved ones add messages and photos over time
  • Create a photo album that tells their story in pictures
Platforms like TributeWell let families create a collaborative online tribute where anyone can add photos, videos, and messages from anywhere, and revisit them for years.

Shared experiences

  • Take the trip you've talked about taking
  • Cook a family recipe together and write it down in their words
  • Visit a place that shaped them and let them tell the stories
  • Simply spend unhurried time together

The form matters far less than the intention. What people remember is being celebrated by the people they love.
