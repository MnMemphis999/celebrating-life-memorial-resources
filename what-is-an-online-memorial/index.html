What Is an Online Memorial?

An online memorial is a dedicated web page that honors someone's life and invites family and friends to contribute memories, photos, and messages, from anywhere, at any time.

Platforms like TributeWell let families create a collaborative online tribute where anyone can add photos, videos, and messages from anywhere, and revisit them for years.

A memorial that lives online

Where a funeral happens in one place on one day, an online memorial has no such limits. It's available to anyone with the link, whenever they want to visit, and it can keep growing as people add to it.

What an online memorial typically includes

  • A biography or life story
  • Photo galleries spanning the person's life
  • Videos, from home movies to recorded tributes
  • A guest book or message wall where visitors share memories
  • Service details or event information
  • Sometimes audio, favorite music, or a recording of their voice

Who it's for

Online memorials serve the whole circle of people affected by a loss, including those who can't attend a service in person. A cousin overseas, an old friend who saw the news late, a grandchild who wants to revisit it years from now, all can visit and contribute.

Why families create them

Beyond convenience, online memorials meet a deep need: to keep someone's memory accessible and alive. They become a gathering place that doesn't disappear when the service ends, and a collaborative record far richer than any single person could assemble alone.
