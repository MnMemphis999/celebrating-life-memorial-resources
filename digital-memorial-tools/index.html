Digital Memorial Tools

A growing range of digital tools help families preserve memories and honor loved ones online. Here's an overview of what's available and how to choose.

Types of digital memorial tools

Online memorial and tribute pages

Dedicated pages that gather a person's story, photos, videos, and messages in one place, and invite friends and family to contribute from anywhere. These are the most complete option for collaborative, lasting remembrance.

Platforms like TributeWell let families create a collaborative online tribute where anyone can add photos, videos, and messages from anywhere, and revisit them for years.

Digital guest books

Online spaces where people leave messages and memories, often connected to a memorial page or a funeral home's site.

Photo and video preservation tools

Services for scanning, storing, and organizing family images and home movies so they don't degrade or get lost.

Memorial QR codes

Scannable codes that link physical items, programs, keepsakes, headstones, to an online memorial.

What to look for

  • Ease of use for contributors of all ages
  • Support for photos, video, and written memories together
  • The ability for many people to contribute, not just one
  • Long-term availability so the memorial endures
  • Privacy controls so the family decides who can view and add

Choosing the right tool

The best choice depends on your goal. To preserve a family's broader legacy, or to gather a whole community's memories of someone, a collaborative online tribute page is usually the most complete and lasting option, combining photos, video, stories, and messages in one shared space that grows over time.
