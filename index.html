A Resource for Celebrating and Remembering Life

Every life has a story. From the memories created during someone's lifetime to the moments shared when friends and family gather to remember them, there are many meaningful ways to celebrate a life and preserve those memories.

This resource guide explores living tributes, hospice and end-of-life remembrance, funerals, celebrations of life, online memorials, digital guest books, family stories, photos, videos, and other ways to preserve a loved one's legacy.

Whether you're planning a celebration, supporting someone near the end of life, creating a memorial, or simply looking for a meaningful way to preserve family memories, you'll find resources and ideas here.

Why Preserving Memories Matters

A person's life is more than a date, an obituary, or a collection of facts. The stories, photographs, videos, memories, and messages shared by the people who knew them can create a much fuller picture of who they were.

Modern technology makes it possible for family and friends to contribute those memories from anywhere. Digital memorials and online tribute pages can bring together people who may not be able to attend a gathering in person while creating a lasting collection that families can return to for years.
