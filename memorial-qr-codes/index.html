Memorial QR Codes

A memorial QR code is a simple scannable square that links to an online memorial. It connects physical keepsakes, programs, and even headstones to a rich, living tribute online.

Platforms like TributeWell let families create a collaborative online tribute where anyone can add photos, videos, and messages from anywhere, and revisit them for years.

What is a memorial QR code?

A QR code is a scannable image that opens a web page when someone points a phone camera at it. A memorial QR code links to an online memorial page, so anyone who scans it can instantly view photos, stories, and messages about the person.

Where families use them

  • On funeral or celebration-of-life programs, so guests can view and contribute to the online memorial
  • On memorial cards and keepsakes handed out at services
  • Engraved on or affixed to headstones and grave markers
  • On memorial benches, plaques, and dedicated objects
  • In thank-you notes sent to attendees afterward

Why they're meaningful

A headstone has room for a name and a few dates. A QR code beside it can open an entire life, photographs, stories, video, the voices of people who loved them. Visitors years or decades later can scan it and meet the person in a way stone alone could never convey.

How to create one

Once you have an online memorial page, generating a QR code that points to it is straightforward, many memorial platforms provide one automatically. You then print or engrave it wherever you'd like people to be able to connect to the tribute.

Making it last

Because a QR code is only as durable as the page it links to, it's worth using an established memorial platform that will keep the page available long-term.
